One recommendation I'd make is to launch in phases instead of trying to build everything at once.

Phase 1: Business directory, restaurant guides, USD/MXN exchange rate, events, and tourism content. These are relatively straightforward to populate and provide immediate value.
Phase 2: Jobs, marketplace/classifieds, and business self-service listings.
Phase 3: User accounts, reviews, messaging, AI assistant, mobile app, and advanced advertising tools.

This phased approach gets the site online sooner, lets you build an audience, and gives you real data about what people use most before investing in more complex features.

AI Search: The Feature That Sets You Apart

I wouldn't make the AI search the internet. I'd make it search only ConectandoEnsenada.org first.

For example:

User: "I'm visiting with my kids this Saturday and have a $100 budget. What should we do?"

The AI could answer by combining information from your own database:

Family-friendly events happening Saturday
Restaurants with kids' menus
Nearby parks or museums
Current promotions from local businesses
Estimated costs
Driving directions between stops

Because it's built from your own listings, guides, events, and business data, every answer keeps users inside your ecosystem while also sending traffic to the local businesses you've partnered with.

One Principle for Claude

When you hand this project to Claude, I'd give it one guiding rule:

Every new feature must make ConectandoEnsenada.org more useful without making it more complicated.

That principle will help keep the platform approachable for first-time visitors while giving you room to grow into the comprehensive digital hub you're envisioning.

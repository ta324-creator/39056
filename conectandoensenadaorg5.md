Instead, think of Google Maps as a discovery source, not your database.

Data Sources

Instead of "Google scraper," I'd define a Business Import Engine that can bring in data from sources you have permission to use or that businesses provide.

Possible inputs include:

Public business directories where licensing allows reuse.
Government or chamber of commerce business registries, if available.
User-submitted businesses.
Businesses claiming and enriching their own listings.
CSV imports for partners.
Future API integrations.

The important idea is that ConectandoEnsenada.org owns and improves its own data over time rather than depending on another site's information.
